# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 21, 2025

This CustomSpeed for Udemy extension respects your privacy. No personal information is collected, transmitted, or stored.

### What data is collected?

None. All settings, such as your chosen playback speed, are stored locally in your browser's storage.

### How is data used?

- Your playback speed preferences are stored and applied only within your browser to enhance your Udemy video experience.
- No data is transmitted outside your device or shared with any third parties.
- All processing happens locally, ensuring your privacy and security.

### Third-party services

This extension does not use any third-party analytics or tracking services.

### Your rights

- You can clear your browser storage at any time to reset all saved preferences.
- You have full control over your data and extension usage.
- You can uninstall the extension at any time.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the support link on the extension page.
